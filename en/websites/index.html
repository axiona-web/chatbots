FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?+
A landing page within 5 working days, a multi-page business website within 10. We confirm the deadline in the quote.
Do I have to write the copy myself?+
No. We write the copy based on information about your business. You simply approve it or send your comments.
What do I need to provide?+
A description of your business and services, your logo if you have one, and contact details. We can add photos from professional stock libraries.
Are the domain and hosting included?+
You pay for the domain yourself (approx. €15/year). Hosting, updates and minor changes are covered by the €19 monthly fee, or we deploy the website on your existing hosting.
Can I edit the website later?+
Yes, after handover the website and all access credentials are yours. We can also make changes for you — as a one-off job or as part of monthly support.
What if I already have a website and just want to improve it?+
We take a look and recommend whether it’s worth improving the existing one or building a new one. We give you honest advice — sometimes a small update is enough.
