This website uses cookies. Necessary cookies are always active. Analytics cookies are only activated with your consent. You can change your settings at any time in the footer of the page.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, phone) when you visit a website. They help the website work properly and give us information about how you use our website.

Which cookies we use

Category Purpose Examples Retention period
Necessary They ensure the basic functionality of the website — e.g. remembering your cookie preferences. cookie_consent 365 days
Analytics They help us understand how visitors use the website — which pages they visit and where they come from. Google Analytics (_ga, _gid) 2 years / 24 hours

Google Analytics

To measure traffic, we use the Google Analytics service provided by Google LLC. This service collects anonymised data about visits (pages, time, device, country). IP addresses are anonymised and are not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

More information: Google Privacy Policy

How to manage cookies

You can change your cookie settings at any time:

  • On this website — click the “Cookies” link in the footer of the page
  • In your browser — you will find the cookie settings in your browser options (Settings → Privacy)
  • Google Analytics — you can opt out via Google Analytics Opt-out

Please note that disabling necessary cookies may affect how the website works.
