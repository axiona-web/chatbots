2. What personal data we collect

We only collect data that you provide to us yourself:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Company name and website (optional)
  • The content of your message or enquiry via the chatbot or contact form

If we approach your company with an offer, we also process business contact details from public sources (company website, business register, professional networks): name, job title, company name, business email and phone number. We will tell you the source on request.

3. Purpose and legal basis of processing

Responding to your enquiry

Legal basis: performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We process the data in order to answer your questions and provide you with a quote.

Legitimate interest

Legal basis: the controller’s legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We use it to keep track of enquiries and to improve our services.

Approaching companies with an offer

Legal basis: the controller’s legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in offering its services to companies for which they may be useful. We only approach business contacts, with an offer related to the company’s activities. We only send offers by email where the law on electronic communications allows it (consent or an existing customer relationship). You can refuse further contact at any time free of charge – simply reply “NO” or write to office@axiona-group.sk.

Cookies

Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We only process analytics and marketing cookies on the basis of your explicit consent.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your data to third parties. We may share it with:

  • Google LLC — Google Sheets for recording enquiries (processor, EU standard contractual clauses)
  • Make.com (Celonis SE) — workflow automation
  • Anthropic, PBC — AI processing of conversations (chatbot)
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — DNS, website protection and transmission of chatbot messages to the AI (Cloudflare Workers)
  • WebSupport s. r. o. — mailboxes and the server that sends messages from contact forms
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting (technical access logs including IP addresses)
  • Google Ireland Limited — Google Analytics, only after your consent to analytics cookies
  • Telegram Messenger Inc. — delivery of notifications

All processors are bound by a data processing agreement or equivalent safeguards under the GDPR. Where a processor transfers data outside the EU (in particular to the USA), this is done on the basis of the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Contact enquiries: 24 months from the last contact
  • Contractual documentation: 10 years (legal obligation)
  • Chatbot logs: 6 months
  • Contacts of companies we have approached: 12 months from the last contact, unless a collaboration begins
  • Request not to be contacted: we keep your email and company name permanently so that we do not contact you again

6. Your rights

You have the following rights:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — you can request the correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can request the deletion of your data (the “right to be forgotten”)
  • Right to data portability — you can receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — you can object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to being approached with offers (direct marketing) at any time and without giving reasons
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can withdraw your consent to cookies at any time

Please send requests to exercise your rights to: office@axiona-group.sk

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic: www.uoou.sk, or with the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work.

7. Cookies

Necessary cookies

They ensure the basic functionality of the website (e.g. storing your consent preferences). They do not require consent.

Analytics cookies

They are used to analyse traffic (e.g. number of visits, behaviour on the website). They are only activated after you give your consent.

Marketing cookies

They are used to display relevant advertising. They are only activated after you give your explicit consent.

You can change your cookie settings at any time by clicking the “Cookies” link in the footer of the page.

8. Security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures — encrypted transmission (HTTPS), restricted access and regular security checks.

9. Changes to this document

We may update this document. We will inform you of significant changes by email or by a notice on the website. The date of the last update is shown at the beginning of the document.
