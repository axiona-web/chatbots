Use cases

AI chatbot use cases

Specific situations, not marketing. Names and companies are anonymised — the scenarios are based on real cases.

🏠 Real estate agency
Situation People interested in flats visited the website in the evenings and at weekends. Agents weren’t available. Website enquiries were getting lost.
Solution A chatbot was deployed — it answers questions about properties and asks about the type of interest, budget and timeframe. The contact is emailed to the agents.
Result
All enquiries outside business hours are captured. In the morning, agents have contacts ready with an overview of each prospect’s interest.
💼 Financial adviser
Situation First calls with clients took a long time — the adviser had to collect basic information the client could have provided in advance.
Solution A chatbot with lead qualification — before the call it asks the prospect about the type of product, timeframe and current contracts. The adviser receives a structured summary.
Result
The adviser goes into the call prepared. The first meeting is shorter and more productive — and the prospect appreciates it, too.
🔧 Local business / service company
Situation Customers asked about prices, availability and appointments — by phone and via the website. Answering took time that should have gone into the work itself.
Solution A chatbot with FAQ — it answers common questions, collects the prospect’s contact details and sends a notification. Appointments are booked via a calendar link.
Result
Fewer interruptions during work. Customers get an answer immediately — even when the owner is out on a job.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about AI chatbots

How long does it take to deploy an AI chatbot?+
We deploy the Basic package within 48 hours of receiving your materials. Standard and Profi take 3–7 days depending on the scope. Custom solutions are designed individually.
How much does an AI chatbot for businesses cost?+
Basic costs €90 setup + €29 per month, Standard €190 + €69 per month and Profi €290 + €129 per month. The monthly fee covers operation, AI and hosting costs, support and minor changes. No lock-in; an annual fee paid in advance = 2 months free.
Do I have to change my website to add the chatbot?+
No. The chatbot is added as a simple script — one line of code. No redesign, no technical work on your side.
Does an AI chatbot work without a CRM?+
Yes. The basic setup works with email and Google Sheets — you don’t need a CRM. CRM integration is an optional extension.
What if I don’t have any materials ready?+
A link to your website and a list of the 5–10 questions customers ask most often are enough. We take care of the conversation structure, answer texts and setup.
Does the chatbot answer only from our data?+
Yes. We set up the chatbot based on your materials — website, services, FAQ, price list. It doesn’t make up answers or provide information from other sources.
What happens if the chatbot doesn’t know the answer?+
The bot politely lets the customer know and offers them the option to leave their contact details or email you directly. It never gives a nonsense answer.
Can I integrate the chatbot with my CRM system?+
Yes. We support integration with Pipedrive, HubSpot, Google Sheets and other tools. CRM integration is available as an optional add-on.
What if I want changes after deployment?+
The first round of changes is included in the price. Further changes — new FAQ, price list updates, flow adjustments — are handled within 24 hours.
Does the chatbot work on mobile?+
Yes. The chatbot is fully responsive and works on all devices — computer, tablet and mobile phone.
How does the chatbot collect customer contacts?+
During the conversation it naturally asks for the customer’s name and email. It then automatically sends the contact to your inbox and saves it to Google Sheets or your CRM.
Is the chatbot GDPR-compliant?+
Yes. The chatbot only processes data that the customer provides voluntarily. Your website includes consent to the processing of personal data in the contact form.
