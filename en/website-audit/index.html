Reality check

What an audit most often reveals

🐢
A slow website on mobile
Every extra second of loading time means visitors who leave before they even see your offer.
🔍
Missing SEO foundations
Poor titles, missing descriptions, pages that aren’t indexed. Google doesn’t show you — but it does show your competitors.
📍
A neglected Google profile
An incomplete business profile, few reviews, outdated opening hours. For local customers, it’s the first — and often the only — impression.
🚪
Visitors don’t know what to do
There’s no clear call to action, the form is hidden or ordering is needlessly complicated. Interest fades away.
Audit scope

What we check

⚡
Speed and Core Web Vitals
Measurements on mobile and desktop using Google’s methodology, with the specific causes of slowdowns.
🔍
SEO foundations
Titles, descriptions, headings, indexing, sitemap and page structure — whatever is stopping Google from finding you.
📍
Google Business Profile
Profile completeness, categories, photos, reviews and consistency of contact details with your website.
🛡
Trust
HTTPS, contact details, references, terms and conditions, GDPR and cookies — the things that decide whether a customer trusts you.
🎯
Conversions
Calls to action, forms and the path to an order. Where visitors drop off, and why.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I get in the free quick audit?+
A one-page summary: how fast your website loads on mobile, the three biggest problems that are probably costing you customers, and what to tackle first. We send it by email within 48 hours.
How long does the full audit take?+
Within 5 working days of your order. The result is a clear PDF with recommendations ranked by impact.
Do you need access to my website?+
No. We carry out the audit using publicly available information — the way customers and Google see your website. If you’d like traffic to be included, you can optionally give us access to Google Analytics.
Can you also fix the problems you find?+
Yes, if you wish. We can prepare a fixed-price quote alongside the audit. The report is written so that your current web provider can use it, too.
Is the audit suitable for a small business or sole trader?+
Yes. Small businesses are the ones that most often lose customers over things that can be fixed quickly and cheaply — and the audit shows them.
