FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What data do you need from me?+
Whatever you have — Excel, Google Sheets, exports from your online shop, invoicing or accounting system. Read-only access or a regular export is enough.
We only have Excel spreadsheets — is that enough?+
Yes. Most of our clients start with spreadsheets. We can build the dashboard on top of them, too.
How often is the data updated?+
Depending on the source — typically daily or in real time. With manual exports, after each upload.
Will anyone else see my data?+
No. The data stays in your accounts, and the dashboard is protected so that only you and the people you choose can access it.
I don’t understand charts and numbers — can you help?+
Yes — we build the dashboard so that it’s easy to understand without any analytics background, and with reporting you also get a written commentary.
