FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which tools can you connect?+
Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, CRM systems (Pipedrive, HubSpot), invoicing tools, forms, social media and hundreds more via the Make.com platform.
Do I need to install anything?+
No. The automations run in the cloud. You only grant access once to the tools you want to connect.
What if my process changes?+
We adjust the automations. Minor changes are covered by the monthly fee; larger ones we price in advance.
Is it secure?+
You grant access through the tools’ official interfaces and can revoke it at any time. Your data stays in your own accounts.
How much time will it actually save me?+
It depends on the process — typically 2 to 10 hours a week. In our proposal we quantify the savings for you.
